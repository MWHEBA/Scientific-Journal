International Journal of Contemporary Management and Information Technology (IJCMIT) (e-ISSN: 2773-5036) is an open access peer reviewed International Journal published by Sciences Gates SDN BHD, Malaysia. The journal aims to publish high quality articles dedicated to management and information technology in the field of management information system (MIS) and management information technology (MIT). Its scope encompasses the applications of management, HRM, Marketing, Finance, IT adoption, MIS, MIT, and others.
