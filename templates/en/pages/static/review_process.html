Peer Review Process

The journal operates a double-blind peer review model to guarantee objective evaluation and maintain the highest academic standards.

Review Stages

  1. Submission: The author submits their manuscript through our online system.
  2. Initial Check: The editor/admin reviews the submission for basic formatting and scope compatibility.
  3. Peer Review: Specialized reviewers assess the paper (typically within 5-10 days).
  4. Decision: Accept, reject, or require revision (limited to a maximum of 2 cycles).
  5. Acceptance & Payment: If accepted, the author receives an invoice to pay the Article Processing Charge.
  6. Publication: The article is automatically published online immediately upon payment verification.

The identities of both reviewers and authors remain completely anonymous throughout the review process.
