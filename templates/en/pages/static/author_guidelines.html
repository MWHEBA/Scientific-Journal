Author Guidelines

Submission Process

Submitting articles is easy and direct — submissions can be made by creating an account on the journal's website or via email at {% if settings.contact_email %} {{ settings.contact_email }}. {% else %} the journal's email address. {% endif %}

  • The researcher is notified of their submission within 24 hours
  • The decision of acceptance or rejection is issued within 3 working days
  • The article status can be tracked from the researcher's dashboard

Submission Requirements

  • The file must be in PDF format with a maximum size of 20 MB
  • The submission must include: Title, Abstract, Keywords, and Scientific Section
  • Abstract: 150–300 words
  • Keywords: 4–8 words separated by commas

Article Formatting

  • Font size: 12pt, double-spaced
  • Margins: 2.5 cm on all sides
  • References: According to APA or IEEE system

Revision Cycle

A maximum of two revision cycles is allowed. After the second cycle, the reviewer must issue a final decision.
