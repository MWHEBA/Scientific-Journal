Publishing Ethics

Authors' Responsibilities

  • Submit original work that has not been published previously.
  • Disclose all co-authors who contributed to the work.
  • Declare all funding sources and potential conflicts of interest.
  • Obtain necessary ethical approvals for research data and images.

Reviewers' Responsibilities

  • Maintain absolute confidentiality regarding all reviewed manuscript materials.
  • Declare any potential conflict of interest prior to initiating review.
  • Provide objective, constructive, and timely peer-review feedback.

Plagiarism Policy

The journal strictly employs software tools to detect plagiarism. Any submitted manuscript found containing plagiarized content will be immediately rejected.
