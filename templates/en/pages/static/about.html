About Us

International Journal of Contemporary Management and Information Technology (IJCMIT) (e-ISSN: 2773-5036) is an open access peer reviewed International Journal published six issues per year (January, March, May, July, September, and November) by Sciences Gates SDN BHD, Malaysia.

The journal aims to publish high quality articles dedicated to management and information technology in the field of management information system (MIS) and management information technology (MIT). Its scope encompasses the applications of management, HRM, Marketing, Finance, IT adoption, MIS, MIT, and others.

IJCMIT allows submission at any time of the year without time restrictions.

Online Submission

To submit a manuscript, please follow these instructions:

  1. Create a new account at Registration Page
  2. Fill in the required information
  3. Choose to submit a new paper
  4. Upload the paper
  5. Submit your paper

Author Guidelines

  1. Author(s) must prepare a full article in English and submit the article to IJCMIT.
  2. Please prepare your article following the journal template.
  3. Referencing style: the author must follow the Numbering referencing style (IEEE). Examples are provided in the template.
  4. After accepting your manuscript, you must fill in the copyright form.
  5. Processing fees should be paid after accepting the manuscript.
